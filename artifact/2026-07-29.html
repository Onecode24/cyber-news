Beginner Pick · Learn the Fundamentals

The MGM Resorts casino hack (2023)

Not breaking news — a foundational case study every newcomer to security should know.

In September 2023, a group of young English-speaking hackers known as Scattered Spider took down MGM Resorts' casino empire not with clever code, but with a phone call. They found an employee's name on LinkedIn, called the company's IT help desk pretending to be that employee, and talked their way into a password reset. That single social-engineering trick got them into MGM's network, where they deployed ransomware that knocked out slot machines, digital hotel room keys, and reservation systems across properties on the Las Vegas Strip for over a week.

MGM refused to pay the ransom and estimated the incident cost it over $100 million in lost business and recovery, while rival Caesars Entertainment, hit by the same group days earlier, reportedly paid roughly $15 million to make the problem go away. The story is a favorite teaching example because the "hack" required almost no hacking skill at all — it's a reminder that people and processes (like how a help desk verifies identity) are often the weakest link, not firewalls or encryption.
