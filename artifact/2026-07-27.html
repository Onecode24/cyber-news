Beginner Pick · Learn the Fundamentals

The Colonial Pipeline attack (2021)

Not breaking news — a foundational case study every newcomer to security should know.

In May 2021, a ransomware gang called DarkSide broke into Colonial Pipeline, which carries almost half the fuel used on the U.S. East Coast, using a single compromised password for an old VPN account that didn't have multi-factor authentication turned on. Colonial shut the pipeline down itself as a precaution, which triggered gas shortages and panic-buying across several states — a striking demonstration of how a digital break-in can ripple into the physical world within days.

The company ended up paying roughly $4.4 million in ransom to get its systems back, though U.S. investigators later clawed back a large chunk of that payment by tracing the cryptocurrency wallet. The incident is a favorite teaching example because it captures three ideas at once: how much damage one weak credential can cause, why MFA on remote access is non-negotiable, and how ransomware has evolved from a nuisance into a threat to essential infrastructure and national security.
